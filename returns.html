Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Our promise

If something isn't right with your order, we'll make it right. You can return most products within 7 days of delivery, as long as they're unused, in their original packaging and with any seals intact. Food and drinks are handled differently (see section 5) because they're perishable.

2. How to start a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at +234 800 000 0000 or use the Contact page.
  2. Share your order number, the item, and (if relevant) a photo or short video of the issue.
  3. We'll confirm whether the item qualifies and arrange pickup or drop-off — returns pickup is free for defective or wrong items.
  4. Once the item reaches us and passes inspection, your refund or replacement is processed.

3. What qualifies

  • Wrong item delivered, or item not as described on the website.
  • Defective, damaged, or dead-on-arrival products (report within 48 hours of delivery with photos).
  • Change of mind on fashion and accessories — unused, unwashed, tags and packaging intact.

What doesn't qualify

  • Items damaged by misuse, accident, or unauthorized repair.
  • Missing packaging, accessories, or components.
  • Hygiene-sensitive items (earbuds, in-ear headphones) once the seal is broken, unless defective.
  • Food, drinks, and other perishables (see section 5).

4. Refunds — how fast

  • Pay on delivery: refunded to your bank account within 3–5 working days of the return passing inspection. We'll ask for your account details on WhatsApp.
  • Bank transfer payments: refunded to the paying account within 3–5 working days.
  • Card payments: refunded to the original card; it may take your bank up to 10 working days to reflect.

Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the return is our fault (wrong, defective, or damaged item). If a defective item is replaced instead of refunded, we cover the replacement delivery.

5. Food & drinks

Because food is prepared fresh for each order, we can't accept returns on taste or preference. But if your order arrives wrong, incomplete, or inedible, contact us within 24 hours with a photo — we'll re-deliver the correct item or refund it in full, your choice.

6. Warranty on electronics

Phones, laptops, and electronics sold with a manufacturer warranty follow the manufacturer's warranty process. Defects within 7 days of delivery are handled directly by us as returns; afterwards, warranty claims go through the brand's service centre, and we'll help you locate it.

7. Questions

WhatsApp or call us at any time — we'll walk you through it.
