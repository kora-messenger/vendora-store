Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Who we are

Vendora is an online store delivering fashion, phones, gadgets, and fresh food in Nigeria. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you browse or order, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Order details — your name, phone number, delivery address, email, order contents, and order notes.
  • Messages — communications you send us on WhatsApp or through the Contact page.
  • Payment confirmation — for bank transfers, your payment confirmation (we never store card details; card payments are processed by our PCI-compliant payment provider).
  • Local storage — your cart and preferences are saved in your browser so they survive page visits.

3. How we use it

  • To confirm, prepare, and deliver your orders.
  • To process refunds, returns, and warranty claims.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To send you offers — only if you subscribe or ask us to. Every message includes how to stop receiving them.
  • To keep the store safe: preventing fraud and misuse.

We never sell your personal data.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use a small cookie/notice system and your browser's local storage to keep your cart working and remember choices like the cookie banner. Analytics, if added later, will always be explained here first. You can clear this data any time in your browser settings.

5. Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to serve you: our delivery partners (your name, address, and phone for the delivery), our payment provider (for card payments), and WhatsApp/Meta as our messaging channel for order confirmations. If the law requires us to share information, we will comply.

6. How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, warranty, and tax purposes. Marketing consent is kept until you withdraw it. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

7. Your rights

  • Ask what data we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data, or stop using it for marketing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these, message us on WhatsApp or use the Contact page — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The store is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security & changes

We store order data securely and limit access to team members who need it. If we change this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the store after changes means you accept the updated policy.
